## Welcome!

I'm excited to launch my new personal website built with **Next.js** and **Tailwind CSS**. This blog will be a place where I share my thoughts on software engineering, project updates, and things I'm learning along the way.

### What to Expect

Here's what you'll find on this blog:

1. **Technical deep-dives** — Exploring interesting problems and solutions
2. **Project updates** — What I'm building and how it's going
3. **Learning resources** — Things I've found helpful in my journey

Here's a quick code snippet to get things started:

### Why I Built This Site

I wanted a space that's:

- **Fast** — Static generation with Next.js means lightning-fast loads
- **Simple** — Markdown files for content, no CMS overhead
- **Mine** — Full control over design and content
